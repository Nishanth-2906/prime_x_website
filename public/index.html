About us

Bangalore-based uPVC manufacturers

We are a Bangalore-based manufacturer specializing in the design and production of premium uPVC windows and doors from last 14 years with excellent track record of on time delivery and never compromised on quality.

Our manufacturing philosophy combines technology, skilled craftsmanship and stringent quality practices to create products that meet the evolving needs of modern architecture.

From selecting quality materials to final product inspection, we focus on every detail to deliver dependable window and door solutions.

Our vision

To become a trusted name in the uPVC windows and doors industry through innovation, quality and customer satisfaction.

Our mission

To manufacture innovative, durable and aesthetically appealing window and door systems that enhance modern living.
